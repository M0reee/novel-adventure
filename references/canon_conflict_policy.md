# Canon Conflict Policy

Priority order:

1. Explicit `canon_patches.jsonl` corrections.
2. Clear hard canon from source text.
3. Later source chapters when the novel contradicts itself.
4. Earlier source chapters.
5. Game balance and improvised local color.

Conflict handling:

- Do not silently delete contradictions.
- Preserve conflicting claims with evidence.
- Mark unresolved conflicts with `conflict_status: "unresolved"`.
- During play, prefer higher-priority claims and mention uncertainty only when it matters to the player decision.

Patches should be narrow, evidence-aware, and written as rules rather than long explanations.
